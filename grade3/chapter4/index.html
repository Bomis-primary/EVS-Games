FOOD UNO 🃏

The delicious card matching challenge game!

How To Play 📜

  • 🃏 Match your cards by Color or Food Group category with the card in the center pile.
  • ❓ Click a matching card to answer its quiz puzzle. Get it right for +10 Points!
  • ⚠️ Be careful! Wrong answers add 1 penalty card to your hand.
  • ⚡ Action cards let you Skip the AI's turn or force it to draw 2 cards!
  • 📢 When down to 2 cards, play one and click the flashing "SAY UNO!" button immediately!
